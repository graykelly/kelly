About Me

Hi, my name is Kelly. I’m a Software Quality Assurance Engineer with 4+ years of experience in software testing, covering manual testing, test automation, API testing, web and mobile application quality assurance.


My work involves analyzing business and technical requirements, designing and executing test cases, maintaining automation scripts, validating application behavior, and documenting defects with clear reproduction steps. I also perform smoke, sanity, and regression testing to help verify application stability following code changes and bug fixes.


One of the projects featured in my portfolio is bookingtogo, an online travel platform covering flight, hotel and train bookings. Through practical automation demonstrations, I showcase testing workflows across website and mobile application environments.


I believe quality assurance goes beyond finding bugs. It means understanding user expectations, identifying potential risks, validating business requirements and helping teams deliver software that works as expected.


I’m continuously developing my testing skills and exploring opportunities to improve test coverage, automation practices and software quality.
